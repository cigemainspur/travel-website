About this journey

Jiangnan tastes as good as it looks. We arrange everything from rustic tea-garden and bamboo-forest dinners to refined Hangzhou cuisine and the imperial Song-dynasty menu.

The highlight is our West Lake yacht gala: a Song-dynasty banquet drifting between the lake lights, with Song poetry chanting and guqin music on board.
