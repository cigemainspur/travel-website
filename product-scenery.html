Classic Sights of Hangzhou

Temples, lakes, shows and tides — the Hangzhou you came for.

Lingyin Temple

About this journey

From the ancient halls of Lingyin Temple to the roaring Qiantang tide, these are the sights that made Hangzhou famous. Each route is led by our senior local guides and paced for comfort.

Choose ready-made classics or let us weave them into a private itinerary — with reserved seats for the best shows and the best photo moments.

Classic experiences

01

Lingyin Temple

China's earliest Buddhist temple and a Jiangnan treasure of grotto art.

02

Sutra Writing at Lingyin

Slow down with brush and ink, and copy scripture in the temple quiet hall.

03

Impressions of West Lake Show

Zhang Yimou's lakeside spectacular with actors dancing on water.

04

Song City Eternal Love Show

Acclaimed as one of the world three great live shows.

05

Longjing Tea Experience

Pick leaves in Hanfu, stir-fry tea the traditional way and brew your own cup.

06

Song Dynasty Tea Whisking

The Song art of whisked tea, where tea becomes painting.

07

Xixi National Wetland Park

The kidney of Hangzhou: tranquil waterways minutes from downtown.

08

Xixi Fisherman's Journey

Pick vegetables, cast nets and taste farmhouse dishes straight from the wetland.

09

Flower Boat Roaming

A private rowing boat with tea snacks, drifting between reeds and flowers.

10

Hupao Spring

One of the Two Wonders of West Lake, beloved by locals for brewing tea.

11

Hangzhou Helicopter Tour

See the Qiantang River, the Lotus stadiums and Liuhe Pagoda from the sky.

12

Qinghefang Imperial Street

The liveliest historic quarter under the old Imperial City.

13

Qiandao Lake

Crystal water and a thousand green islands, best enjoyed from a small boat.

14

Qiantang River Tide Watching

Witness the world-famous one-line tide from the best spot in Yanguan.
