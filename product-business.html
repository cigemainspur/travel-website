About this journey

Hangzhou is not only West Lake — it is also the birthplace of Alibaba and one of China's leading AI hubs. Our business inspection tours bring delegations face to face with smart manufacturing, autonomous driving and frontier research.

Every visit is arranged with official access, professional interpretation and flexible half-day to multi-day modules, so your delegation gets the most out of every stop.
