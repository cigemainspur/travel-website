About this journey

From a single day immersing in a Song-Dynasty water town, to a ten-day discovery of Hangzhou, Suzhou, Wuzhen and Wuxi — every itinerary is built from scratch by a dedicated planner who works with you from the first message to the final farewell.

Our private journeys include a local host, private transport, hand-picked boutique stays, and 24/7 on-call support. Whether you want a quiet temple retreat, a food-focused weekend, or a multi-generational family adventure, we design the trip around what makes you happy.
