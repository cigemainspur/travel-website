Cultural Experience

About this journey

Our cultural experiences turn visitors into participants. Walk a Song-Dynasty street in period costume as NPCs thread a story around you, sit down to a banquet your ancestors would recognise, then carry home a sachet you stitched yourself.

Four modular tracks let you build your own cultural journey: Roleplay scripts, Palace banquets & cuisine, Museum visits with curators, and DIY workshops (tea, fan, pastry, sachet). Pick one, or combine them all.

Four cultural modules

A

Roleplay Scripts

Costumed NPCs, a plot, and a whole day of immersive story. Past favourites include "24 Hours Back to the Song Dynasty" and "Dating in Song Town".

B

Palace Banquets & Cuisine

Six dining styles: tea-garden dinner, bamboo-forest dinner, Song-dynasty menu, Hangzhou local, vegetarian, and dim sum.

C

Museum Visits

Curator-led tours of Sword Museum, Fan Museum, Tea Museum, Zhejiang Museum (Zhijiang Hall), China Silk Museum, Liangzhu Museum and more.

D

DIY Workshops

Southern-Song dim-sum making, local pastry craft, traditional Chinese-medicine sachet sewing, Hangzhou fan painting.
