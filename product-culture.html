Hu Qing Yu Tang Pharmacy

About this journey

Our cultural workshops turn visitors into participants. Learn brush calligraphy from a master, sew a herbal sachet with a TCM pharmacist, or try on opera costume for a stage rehearsal.

Every experience is hosted by inheritors and professionals of intangible cultural heritage, in small groups, with English and Russian interpretation available.
