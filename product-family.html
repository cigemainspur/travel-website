About this journey

Family travel with kids is a different art form — slower pacing, hands-on activities, safe transport, and food that even picky eaters will love. Our family itineraries are designed around the child, so parents actually get to relax.

From pastry-making workshops where children craft Song-Dynasty sweets, to tea-picking adventures in Longjing, to curator-led museum hunts — every moment is built to spark curiosity and create shared memories across generations.

What makes a family trip with us different

★

Kid-friendly pacing

Long lunches, mid-day rests, and zero overnight drives. We design around your child's rhythm.

★

Hands-on workshops

Pastry-making, tea-picking, fan-painting and sachet-crafting — kids come home with their own Jiangnan souvenir.

★

Vetted guides

Our family-trip guides are first-aid trained, fluent in English/Chinese, and great with kids from 3 to 17.

★

Family-friendly stays

Family rooms, kids' meals, swimming pools and stroller access — we only recommend what we'd book for our own.
