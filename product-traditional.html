Classic Tours

About this journey

Our classic itineraries are designed for first-time visitors who want to see the essentials without the hassle of planning. Cover the icons of Hangzhou and Jiangnan in 1 to 7 days, with small groups and expert local guides.

From a leisurely loop around West Lake, to temple-hopping at Lingyin, to a morning among Longjing's terraced tea fields — these are the routes that have been refined over decades of family-run travel in Hangzhou.

Classic routes

1d

West Lake & Hefang Street

A leisurely loop around West Lake, broken up with a stroll through Hefang's old tea houses and souvenir lanes.

2d

Lingyin & Longjing

Lingyin Temple at dawn, then a slow morning among the tea terraces — tasting the freshest Longjing.

3d

Xixi Wetland & Song Town

Boat through Xixi Wetland, then a half-day at Song Dynasty Town — the open-air museum of Jiangnan.

5d

Hangzhou + Wuzhen

The above, plus an overnight in the water town of Wuzhen — boats, lanterns and quiet canals.
